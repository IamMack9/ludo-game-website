Privacy Policy

Ludo Master X

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ludo Master X. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our mobile game.

Ludo Master X is designed primarily as an offline Ludo game. We respect your privacy and aim to handle information responsibly.

2. Information We Collect

Ludo Master X does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or password to play the game.

The game does not require online multiplayer or a personal user account for its core gameplay.

3. Advertising

Ludo Master X may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service.

Depending on applicable settings, permissions, and Google's services, advertising-related information may include device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location information, and information related to advertisements and interactions with advertisements.

For more information about Google's practices, please review Google's applicable privacy and advertising policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Ludo Master X may use third-party services such as Google AdMob to provide advertisements.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Offline Gameplay

Ludo Master X is primarily designed for offline gameplay. Core gameplay does not require an online account or online multiplayer connection.

6. Children's Privacy

Ludo Master X does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that personal information has been provided unintentionally, please contact us so that we can consider appropriate action.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
