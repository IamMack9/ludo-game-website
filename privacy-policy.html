Privacy Policy

Ludo Master X

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ludo Master X. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our mobile game.

Ludo Master X is designed as an offline Ludo game. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only information that is necessary for providing, improving, and supporting the app.

Ludo Master X does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or password to play the game.

The game does not require online multiplayer or a personal user account.

Advertising-related information may include device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location information, and information related to advertisements and interactions with them, depending on Google's services and applicable settings.

For more information, please review Google's privacy policy and advertising policies.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for displaying advertisements.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Offline Gameplay

Ludo Master X is primarily designed for offline gameplay. Core gameplay does not require an account or online multiplayer connection.

6. Children's Privacy

Ludo Master X is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us unintentionally, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.
