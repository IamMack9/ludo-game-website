About Ludo Master X

Ludo Master X is a mobile Ludo game created for players who enjoy the classic board game in a modern digital experience.

The game focuses on simple controls, colourful gameplay, smooth token movement and an enjoyable experience for friends, family and solo players.

Ludo Master X is designed primarily for offline gameplay and does not require an account for its core game experience.
